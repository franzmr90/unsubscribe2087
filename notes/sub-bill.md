# A subscription bills the credits on the line

The items sheet prints the minute’s bill with `fmt`, which is whole credits. The purse was paying the fraction, and a purse sitting on the printed number let the subscriptions lapse.

At prices ×1.073 one running subscription is 12.876 and the line says ₡12. Twelve credits now cover it, and the purse pays 12. Two running ones are ₡25. Eleven credits still cannot cover the ₡12 bill, and the subscriptions lapse.
