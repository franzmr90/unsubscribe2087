# Inventory, the lockbox, and salvage

Read against `skipPick`, the lockbox move, `craft`, the satoshi roll in `kill`, and `useObj` for a chest.

## What already matched

Standing on a drop picks it up after a third of a second. Credits, sats, and words come in from a little farther than gear. The action button is how you take a piece the filter left. An empty slot is always filled. Identified gear that is not an upgrade is what Better-only leaves behind. Unidentified gear has no comparison, so that filter does not leave it.

The pack is 24. The lockbox is 36, shared by every hero, and deleting a hero does not touch it. Sats go in and come out as the same number. Credits are stored as what they bought that day, and the deposit keeps nine tenths. Scrap pays 1, 2, 4, 8, or 15 parts by quality, and the button prints that number. A subscription scraps for 2. Cutting a socket costs 12. Selling pays two fifths of the price, and bulk sell does not take what you are wearing or a running subscription.

Champions are a 35% satoshi roll for 3–6. Lieutenants always drop 8–12. The table in the Codex is computed from the same weights the drops use. Items land at the machine’s level or up to two above.
