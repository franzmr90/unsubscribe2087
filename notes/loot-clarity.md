# Inventory, the lockbox, and salvage

Read against `skipPick`, the lockbox move, `craft`, the satoshi roll in `kill`, and `useObj` for a chest.

## What already matched

Standing on a drop picks it up after a third of a second. Credits, sats, and words come in from a little farther than gear. The action button is how you take a piece the filter left. An empty slot is always filled. Identified gear that is not an upgrade is what Better-only leaves behind. Unidentified gear has no comparison, so that filter does not leave it.

The pack is 24. The lockbox is 36, shared by every hero, and deleting a hero does not touch it. Sats go in and come out as the same number. Credits are stored as what they bought that day, and the deposit keeps nine tenths. The lines are whole credits: half of 101 is 50, the box shows ₡45, and take-all pays 45. All of 101 shows ₡90 and pays 90. Scrap pays 1, 2, 4, 8, or 15 parts by quality, and the button prints that number. A subscription scraps for 2. Cutting a socket costs 12. Selling pays two fifths of the price, in the whole credits the button prints, and bulk sell does not take what you are wearing or a running subscription.

Champions are a 35% satoshi roll for 3–6. Lieutenants always drop 8–12. The table in the Codex is computed from the same weights the drops use. Items land at the machine’s level or up to two above.

## What the cards printed

The pickup hint said unidentified gear is still picked up. A struck-out tier is left on the ground with the name hidden or not. Better-only is the rule that skips the judgment. Subscriptions are picked up even when Magic is struck out. The hint now says those three things, and the action button is still how a left piece comes up.

The lockbox said an item still needs its level, under the line about moving it across. The move does not look at the level. Wearing it does. The line now says wearing.

Equipping a subscription toasted that charms work from the pack. They run on the subscription row, and the item itself already says they are not in the pack. The toast now says they are not worn.

Golden Handshake said every machine has a 10% chance. The roll is 13%, and only for an ordinary machine. Champions, lieutenants, and executives keep their own chances. The glove now says ordinary machines, 13%.

The Codex named Pryce at 21 and a lockbox at 30%. Vale pays 42. The other executives pay 21. The 30% roll, for 2–5 sats, is the lockbox you open in a district. The rig’s chest is the other lockbox, and it does not make that roll.

A belt worth 10 at prices ×1.15 sells for 4.6, and the button said ₡4 while the purse gained 4.6. Two of them said ₡9, the floor of 9.2, while each line was ₡4. The purse now gains the whole credits on the line: 4 for one, 8 for the pair. A subscription uses the same price.

A credit pile prints the same way. A pile of 10.6 shows +₡10 and used to add 10.6. With 40% extra credits that pile is 14.84 and the line said +₡14. The purse now gains the whole credits on the line: 10, or 14 with the extra. A pile that floors to nothing adds nothing and does not flash +₡0.
