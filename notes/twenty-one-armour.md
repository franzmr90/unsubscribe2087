# Twenty-One Million armour

The Maximalist passive Twenty-One Million (skill 8) prints `+8% armour` per rank. Damage already gained `+3%` per rank, and a high rank already cancels the difficulty divisor on a hit. Armour did not move.

`calc` now multiplies armour by `1 + 0.08 × rank`, the same way Iron Hide does. Rank 10 still adds the two mastery ranks every other passive gets. Switching the passive off removes it. A Courier with Deadeye, also skill 8, does not take it.
