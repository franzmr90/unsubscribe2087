# A resting bid holds the credits the line names

A posted bid prints the credits it holds with `fmt`. The purse was setting aside the fraction, and a purse that held exactly the printed amount could not post.

At a mid price of 103, a bid 3% under is 99.91. The line says ₡99. The purse now sets aside 99, and withdrawing the order returns 99. The limit stays 99.91, so the order still fills at the price you named. The cost kept for that fill is the credits that were held, less the rebate.
