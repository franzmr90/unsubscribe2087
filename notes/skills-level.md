# Skill points, the level, and the attributes

Read against `skCost`, `gainXp`, `webPts`, `paraNeed`, Sister Null’s unlearn, and `shapeShift`.

## What already matched

A level gives one skill point and five attribute points, and it fills life and energy. Ranks 1 through 5 cost one point. Ranks 6 through 10 cost two, and the button says so. Rank 10 adds two ranks of effect, and an active skill recovers 20% faster. Might, Reflex, Vitality, and Bandwidth add 1.5% damage, 0.3% deadly strike and 1 armor, 4 life, and 3 energy, before the percentage multipliers on life and armor. Bandwidth also puts 0.05 energy back each second, on top of the base 4. That second part is still unprinted. The cards for the four attributes were left as they are.

The web takes one point for each level gained and one for each Tenure. Level 1 has none. Taking the web back costs 5 sats and clears every node. A doctrine is free the first time and 3 sats after that, in town. Passives swap in town. The rig costs 5 sats. Quest lines that say +1 skill point or +5 attribute points grant that.

## What the cards printed

Sister Null said your points were returned, and the rig said every point comes back. Both added up ranks. A skill at rank 10 cost 15 points and came back as 10. The return now uses the same two-point cost the button charges, and a random rig spends that cost too, so it cannot buy the deep ranks at one point each.

The web said one point per level. It is one point each time you gain a level. The level toast now names the web point beside the skill point and the five attribute points.

Tenure said each one costs 7% more experience than the first. The first costs half a level. Each later one costs 7% of that half more than the one before it. The +1% damage and life, and the machines growing a little, were already true.

At the level cap, Acts II through IV said you had reached it. The cap still rises: 25, then 50, 75, 90, and 100 in Act V. Only Act V now says the limit is reached. The skills page names the fifth passive slot, which opens in Act V.

A skill under another one claimed +6% from the one above even when that skill does not scale. The line shows only where the skill actually takes the bonus, and it says so.

The Caretaker’s reward line did not mention the 3 attribute points the turn-in gives either way. The credits are still the part you can refuse.
