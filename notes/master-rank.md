# A mastered skill’s card uses the extra ranks

Rank 10 adds two ranks in `sl` and makes an active skill recover in four fifths of the time. The card’s “Now” line and its recovery used the ten ranks you spent.

Iron Hide at 10, switched on, is 12 ranks: +96% armor and +48% life, and life is 1.48 times the unranked pool. The card says that. Stop Sign’s recovery prints 0.64s, four fifths of 0.8s. At 9 points the next line already includes the two extra ranks. At 5 points the card stays on the rank you have.
