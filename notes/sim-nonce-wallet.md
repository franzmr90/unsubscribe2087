# VR Simulator, Nonce’s market, and the seed wallet

Read against `simTick`, `rushTick`, `rushList`, `socketWord`, `gotWord`, `satShop`, and `satPrice`.

## What already matched

The rig’s own note is the one the code follows. Waves grow, a lieutenant joins when the wave number divides by five, experience is paid on the kill at three fifths, credits drop at the hero’s feet after every wave but the first, and a lockbox drops when a fifth wave has just been cleared. Kills inside the rig do not drop gear, sats, or words. Falling there takes no fee and puts you back in the act’s town at full life. The gear you were wearing stays.

Nonce’s table is restocked in town as rare, set, and unique only, and the buy button spends sats. Credit prices on Brenda’s counter climb with the index. A stim priced in sats stays near the same count, because the fair price of a sat tracks that index. Rank 1 with the Bitcoiners (`satPrice` at 10 reputation) is nine tenths of the printed sat price. Rank 2 finds a quarter more sats on a pickup. The spread tightens a little at every rank, which is what the rank-3 line is describing by the time you reach it.

White weapons socket about two times in five, and some blue ones socket once. Yellow, green, and gold do not come socketed. A word in a socket always adds its own bonus. A phrase happens only when the sockets are full and the words are one of the six listed orders. Finding a word marks it found. Socketing spends the copy in your pack and does not un-find it. Nonce opens the wallet at twelve found words, and pays 210 sats.

## What the cards printed

The advanced walkthrough said “a lockbox” with no cadence, and “there are 10 more” after the two-sat purse. The second of those reads as ten more fights. The rush adds ten sats once, and the list does not grow.

It also said every lieutenant you have already beaten. `rushList` includes a lieutenant when you have walked out of that district, or when that lieutenant is already down, and then the executives of acts you have finished. Walking past one is enough.

The twelve-word slide said setting the words in, in order, makes a phrase, and that you take the notes to Nonce. The order has to be the right words. The wallet does not look in your pack.

The Codex seed line and the More tab on the Transit Gate said the same two things. They now use the walkthrough’s wording. The scene after the wallet stays as it was: that line is about the phrase on the old wallet, and the button already opened because the twelve words had been found.

The market slide was left as printed. “Those prices stay” is the sat price of his goods and of a stim, which is the sentence under the sats sheet.
