# Walkthrough

Two short decks on the scene player the acts already use. Opening one pauses the world the way a cinematic does. Closing it returns to play, or to the title if that is where it was opened. Nothing in combat is locked.

## Where it opens

- Title, first row of the wanderer list: Walkthrough, in gold, above the saved heroes and the empty slots. The same button stays under the class list. Basics, Advanced, or Close.
- Options, the first button under the heading: the same chooser. The sheet closes first, and Done puts you back in the district.
- Once, after a new wanderer first reaches a town that is not the prequel. The line is Before you go. It says left click walks and strikes, right click casts, and that the guide can be opened again from the top of the character list, from Options, or with H. Basics, Advanced, or Not now. A checkbox, Don’t show this again, is stored on the options (`OPT.noWalk`) and applies to later heroes too.

The prompt is armed only by `G.walkNew`, which `newGame` sets. A save that never had that flag is not asked. Dismissing the prompt clears it for that hero. Checking the box means a later new hero is not asked either. Title and Options still open the decks.

Back is on the deck, hidden on the first slide. The last button says Done. Story scenes still end on Begin. Skip still leaves the whole deck.

## Tracks

Basics (9): the mouse and the stick, the swing, the HUD, a level, where a skill sits, gear, quests, town, death.

Advanced (9): the VR Simulator, boss rush, Nonce’s market, seed words and the wallet, magic find, hirelings, party and hostility (the Party tab only while the account server is connected), doctrine plus Story and Codex, difficulty, hardcore, and thin effects.
