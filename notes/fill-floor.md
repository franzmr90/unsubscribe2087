# A filled order pays the credits the line names

A fill prints the rebate with `fmt`, and a sale's price the same way. The purse was gaining the fraction.

An ask posted 3% over a mid of 100.4 rests at 103.412. The fill says ₡103 and a rebate of ₡0. The purse now gains 103. A bid that rested at 800.6 pays a rebate of 1.601; the line says ₡1, and the purse gains 1. The limit that decides when the order fills is unchanged.
