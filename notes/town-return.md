# Town, death, and the way back

The death screen, the walkthrough, and the declaration hold were read against `enter(HT())`, `G.portal`, and `mpPortalBlocked`.

## What already matched

A town portal (the Portal button, or T) saves where you are standing and opens in the square of the act's town, at 12.5, 11.5. It will not open with a machine within four tiles, and it will not open in town. Walking a district door uses the label on the button: home is the act town, back is the previous district or that town, on is the next district. The re-onboarding fee on the death screen is the number already taken. The notice prints whole credits, so the charge is the floor of the rate: ninety credits at five percent is ₡4, and three hundred thirty-three at fifteen percent is ₡49.

## What the screen printed

The button always said Rise in Stillwater. `HT()` is Stillwater Commons in Act I, then The Landing, The Antechamber, Cutler Avenue, and The Garage. The walkthrough said the portal returns you to the spot. Death stores `W.start`, the entrance of the district, and the blue marker in the square walks you there.

Hardcore still showed the portal sentence and the Stillwater button. That path deletes the hero and starts the prequel over. The note now says there is no portal, and the button says Begin again.

A player hit is allowed while a dialog is up. It was also allowed during the fall, so a second blow ran the death again: another fine, another count, and the notice started over with the new name. While you are already falling, that blow does not land.

## The declaration

Declaring holds you for ten seconds. Doors in the world already refused. The Transit Gate, the VR rig, the prequel replay, and a town portal opened from a district did not. They now use the same refusal: "The declaration holds you here for a moment."
