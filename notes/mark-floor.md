# Marked to Market prints the 30% it already deals

The card said +20% and +4% per rank. A marked machine already takes +30% from Orange Pill, Signed For, and Dox, and this aura uses that same mark. Rank 1 and rank 2 stay at 30%. Rank 3 is +32%, and the card’s number from there on.

The radius is unchanged. The aura does not deal less than the other marks.
