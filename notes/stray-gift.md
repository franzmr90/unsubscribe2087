# A stranger takes whole credits

Helping a stranger with no stim costs fifteen percent of your credits. The line says "You give up" and then `fmt` of that amount, which is whole credits. The purse was losing the fraction: ninety credits printed ₡13 and lost 13.5, and three hundred thirty-three printed ₡49 and lost 49.95.

The gift is now the floor of fifteen percent, which is the number on the line. A stim, when you have one, is still the gift instead.
