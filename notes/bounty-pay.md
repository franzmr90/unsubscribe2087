# A bounty pays the credits on the board

The board and the “Paid” line use `fmt`, which is whole credits. The purse was gaining the fraction.

A contract of 120 at prices ×1.073 is 128.76. The board says ₡128. The purse now gains 128, and the paid line says ₡128.

Anon standing still multiplies the contract first. At the first rank that is 150, which floors to ₡160. The board keeps posting the contract before that bonus.
