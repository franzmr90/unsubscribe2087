# A price tag is what you pay

Brenda, Wren, Kestrel, Sister Null, and Mr. Float print a credit price with `fmt`, which is whole credits. The purse was paying the fraction, and a purse sitting on the printed number could not complete the purchase.

At prices ×1.073 and level 1, a stim is 32.19 and the tag says ₡32. Appraisal is ₡42, a mystery box is ₡125, a hire is ₡268, and both the five-minute strength and unlearning are ₡160. Each of those is now the amount the purse pays. The button turns on when you hold that many credits.

The market still samples the raw stim price. A late re-onboarding cap still uses the raw hire price, and that fee was already floored on its own.
