# A quote pays the credits the line names

Nonce prints the buy and the sell with `fmt`, which is whole credits. The purse was moving the fraction, and the buy button stayed shut when you held exactly the printed amount.

At a mid price of 100, one sat costs 100.877 and sells for 99.127. The lines say ₡100 and ₡99. The purse now moves those floors. The price, the fee, and the walk down the book are unchanged, so a large order still fills for less than the headline.
