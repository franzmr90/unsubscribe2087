# Nonce’s goods are thirty percent off Brenda’s price

Below a social credit of 500, Nonce stocks the gear Brenda will not sell. The button, his line, and the hero sheet called that price “at cost.” The charge was seven tenths of her price.

Her price is the whole credits she already prints. His is thirty percent off that number, floored again. A value of 40 at inflation 1 is ₡100 from her and ₡70 from him. A value of 13 at inflation 1.1 is ₡35 from her and ₡24 from him. The old product of the unrounded price would have charged ₡25 for that second one.
