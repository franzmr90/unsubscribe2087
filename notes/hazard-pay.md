# Hazard Pay names the credits it pays

A throw that kills adds `(3 + rank) × inflation` to the purse. The card printed `3 + rank` and left inflation off, so after prices moved the line was short of the purse.

The card now prints the whole credits, `floor((3 + rank) × inflation)`. The purse gains that same floor. Magic find stays `6 × rank` for the drop, then clears. A dash, a simulator kill, and a kill that was not a throw still pay nothing. At inflation 1 the number is still `3 + rank`.
