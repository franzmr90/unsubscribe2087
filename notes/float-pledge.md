# Mr. Float's pledge matches the line

The collateral offer prints a pledge of at least ten sats, whole credits, and a call price. The button was pledging the trade size instead, so a size of one still said ten and then took one. The call on the offer was the loan divided by the sats and by the liquidation ratio. The loan itself calls the position when one sat falls under the bid times that ratio.

The button now pledges the printed count. The purse gains the printed floor, and paying him back costs the floor of the owe line. The call on the offer is the same threshold the loan stores.
