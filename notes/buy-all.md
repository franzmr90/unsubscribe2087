# Buy everything spends the quote

Nonce’s “Buy with everything” turned on once the purse held the headline ask, then refused. The count it used stopped eight percent short of the quote, so a purse that could buy the one sat on the line still heard that it could not.

The button now stays shut until that quote fits, and the click buys the most sats the quote will sell for the credits in hand. The price, the fee, and the walk down the book are unchanged.

At a mid price of 100, one sat still costs ₡100. Holding that printed amount used to leave the button live and the toast saying not enough. It now buys the one sat and the purse pays ₡100. One credit under keeps the button shut. A larger purse buys every sat the quote will fill, not the short count.
