# A loan pays the credits the line names

Mr. Float’s borrow line uses `fmt`, which is whole credits. The purse was gaining the fraction, and the repay line charged it back.

At prices ×1.073 the loan is 429.2. The line says ₡429. The purse now gains 429. The fee is still ten percent of that, 471.9, and the repay line says ₡471. Paying ₡471 clears it. Interest still compounds on the balance between visits.
