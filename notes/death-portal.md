# A death notice already owes you the portal

Softcore death tells you a portal back to the district entrance will be waiting in the square. That portal was written only when you tapped Rise.

Hiding the page on the notice saves the hero. The save is at 0 life, and loading a softcore 0-life save wakes you in town and drops the district you died in. The portal was never on the hero, so the square had no way back.

The portal is written when the hero falls, at the district entrance, the same place Rise uses. A save during the notice keeps it. Hardcore still writes no portal; that hero does not come back.
