# The Stipend counts whole stims

Brenda sells a stim for the whole credits on the tag. The Stipend said the base ₡500 buys `500 / stim price`, printed to two decimals, so a ₡30 stim was “16.67 stims.”

The line now counts how many of those tags ₡500 pays for. At level 1 and inflation 1 that is 16. At inflation 1.07 the tag is ₡32 and the line says 15. The deposit itself is unchanged.
