# A covered node does not say it costs 5 sats

Lighting a node takes 5 sats. Below a social credit of 500, Sister Null’s people put those 5 back, so the purse does not move. The action button still said “5 sats.”

Below 500 the button says free. At 500 and above it still says 5 sats, and a purse short of 5 still cannot light it.
