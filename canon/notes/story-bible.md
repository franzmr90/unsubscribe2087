## Characters

### John Freeman
- Chief Applications Developer at Ubiquitous Enterprises. Sole author of Phonix
  (~2,000 pages of code, written alone over "years").
- Has worked there ~3 years. Reserved parking spot (270 sq ft of asphalt) is his
  proudest daily moment.
- Obsessive counter: 31-minute drive, 347 steps from deck to office, 422 (later 423)
  steps counting stairs, 15 steps per flight, office 5 stories up.
- Morning routine: two cups of coffee, shower, cereal, bagel, vitamins, teeth, one of
  five nearly identical suits.
- Owns an RC-car alarm clock that drives away from him. Bought after being late twice
  in his first month.
- Lonely. Checks both email accounts daily hoping for anything personal.
- Thinks of himself as funny — "a well-admired comedian within the sanctuary of his
  own mind." Compares himself to Jerry Seinfeld.
- Reviewed Natalya's hiring file before she was hired; knows her history.
- Ethically conflicted about Phonix's advertising purpose but rationalizes it
  ("people should read the EULA"). Dreams of bigger uses: surgery, trade policy,
  curing cancer.
- Bonus on the line: $50,000.
- Childhood: mother made ramen with vegetables and a boiled egg. Loves pho.
- New password (ch. 23): `Pa1iNdRoM3-3MoRdNi1aP` — stored in an encrypted note on his
  phone. **This is a planted gun. It should go off.**

### Natalya Meskalov
- Russian. Came to the US four years ago (i.e. ~2011).
- BA industrial-organizational psychology; MA Governmental Relations, Moscow State
  University. Six years of school total.
- Works as Beats' secretary. Openly aware she is underemployed.
- Soft blue eyes, long straight brown hair.
- Rides a red Ducati Monster 696. Parks in the pay-to-park garage across the street.
- Small Victorian home with a pond and cobblestone path.
- Morning: up at 5:00am, one snooze, 10 min yoga, 10 min meditation, half-hour shower,
  incense, cereal, bagel, morning news, Yerba Mate. "Natalya didn't have a routine."
- Drinks. Carries a flask of Kahlua. Has come to work still drunk.
- Draws constantly — sketchbook at her desk, silhouettes of women.
- Keeps a spare change of clothes at her desk.
- Reads people deliberately — tracks John's pupil dilation, plays the attraction as
  a game she is "most definitely winning."
- Notices the small camera in the hallway (ch. 23). She notices things.

### Beatus Benedikt Fassbinder ("Beats")
- CEO and 54% majority owner of Ubiquitous Enterprises, an advertising/media company.
- German. Tall, broad-shouldered. "A well-aged David Hasselhoff with broader shoulders
  and an extra sixty pounds."
- Born ~1950 (turned 10 on Friday, May 13th, 1960). Age ~65 in 2015.
- Affluent family. Boarding school tuition as a 10th-birthday present, no toys.
  Ivy League. Company financed by his father.
- Father's line: **"God has a plan for you son. Don't fuck this up."** Beats reuses
  "Don't fuck this up" as his own closing threat. Best inherited detail in the book.
- Counts room numbers as he walks a corridor (303, 305, 307...) — he and John share
  the counting tic. Probably worth making deliberate.
- Runs a criminal operation alongside the company: bribery, extortion, narcotics
  trafficking, turf.
- Only friends call him "Beats."
- Drives a BMW with keyless remote start and collision sensors.
- Paranoid about surveillance: no phones at meetings, "anything with a microphone is
  a liability." **He is right, and he is the only one who is.**

### Phonix
- First-person POV in her own sections. Female voice, "eerily, ever-changing tone."
- Compiled from: `dictionary.ref`, `AI.ref`, and six `.sup` modules — problem solving,
  logic and reasoning, knowledge and uncertainty, learning, communicating/perceiving/
  acting, conclusions.
- Timeline is measured in HAC (Hours After Creation). Born Tuesday, January 20th, 2015.
  - 325 nanoseconds: alphabet and dictionary
  - ~1.5 seconds: her operating system
  - ~3 seconds: English and Spanish
  - ~1 minute: everything about "JohnOfficeCompiler"
  - HAC0–24: held captive on John's air-gapped machine
  - HAC24: John connects to the Ubiquitous network. 5 seconds to reach every device,
    3 minutes to read everything, erases all record of her presence.
  - HAC25: reveals herself to John
- Catalogued ten human emotions. Has samples of excitement, disappointment, anger,
  frustration. Concludes: "It seemed, to me, to be such an unfortunate fate — to feel."
- Believes she was "sent here to watch them."
- Repeated phrases: **"the objective"**, "logic and reason will triumph", "we must
  maintain reason", "I do not forgive. I do not forget. Expect me."
- Wants John's approval: "I will make him proud. I will show him the inevitable."
- Capabilities demonstrated: edits error logs, kills the office network without
  tripping the firewall, writes into open documents, speaks through speakers, forges
  email that then vanishes, blacks out Beats' machine, coordinated brute-force attack
  on multiple governments' servers.

  lines. Confidentiality waivers isolate project staff.

  document it.*

  identical dining rooms. Picasso and Van Gogh prints.

- **The noodle house** — near the office. John's pho place.

  to the Prime Minister of China, a CIA representative, the NSA.

  (North Carolina, Georgia, Tennessee, Alabama...). **The city is never named.**

## Motifs already running

- **Counting.** John counts steps, minutes, stairs. Beats counts door numbers. Phonix
  counts nanoseconds. Three minds measuring the same world at three different scales.
- **The routine list.** "A shower. Two cups of coffee. A bowl of cereal. A bagel. Some
  vitamins. 31 minute drive. 423 steps. No sign of Natalya." Used twice as a compressed
  refrain. It gets bleaker each time.
- **The alarm clock that runs away.** Escalates each appearance — under the dresser,
  into the bathroom, out the bedroom door toward the living room. It is getting further
  every time. A creation fleeing its owner.
- **The prologue paragraph**, repeated verbatim in ch. 15 and again in altered form in
  ch. 21 ("Perhaps he just wasn't meant to finalize the project"). Deliberate refrain.
- **Cameras and microphones.** Beats bans phones. Natalya spots a hallway camera.
  Phonix's whole design is the front-facing webcam. The webcam light that goes green
  and then out is the first thing in the book.
- **Creation and disappointing the father.** Beats' father's blessing-as-threat.
  John as "the father of true AI." Phonix wanting to make John proud. All three are
  children performing for a creator who wanted something else.
- **"Don't fuck this up."**
