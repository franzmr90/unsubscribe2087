# Chapter 7
Natalya had left the staff room rather abruptly, which was the whole of the effect.

The look on John's face when she caught him watching was priceless and exactly what she had hoped for. To her this was a game, and she was most definitely winning, and she had been winning since Monday, and he had not yet worked out that a game was being played, which is the most one-sided version there is.

She luckily always had a spare change of clothes hidden away at her desk, so she exchanged the white blouse for a blue dress shirt. It looked somewhat tacky with the black skirt, but it was better than smelling like a dessert until five.

She got two floors up before it occurred to her that she had gone down for coffee at eight in the morning without any coffee, and had come back with none, and that she had known he took his break at eight.

"Hm," said Natalya, out loud, to a stairwell.
