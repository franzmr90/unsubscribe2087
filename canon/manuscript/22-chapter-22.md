# Chapter 22
Beats stumbled into the parking deck and tread heavily across the concrete, his mind aflame with fury.

"Who the hell has the audacity to tell me what to do?" Beats scoffed. "I'll show this fucker."

Beats had friends in the intelligence community. He had tapped people's phones and traced them before. Who needs a warrant when you have connections? He was sure he could hunt down whoever was doing this, challenging his empire. He would make them regret the day they crossed his will.

It did not once occur to him, then or in the eleven years afterward, to wonder why a man in his position had friends in the intelligence community, or what he had ever done for them.

Beats unlocked and started his BMW via remote. The car knew he was inside when the radio transmitter crossed the door threshold. He zoomed out of the garage and into the street. A sensor beeped and flashed on his dash to warn him of a near collision, and someone honked their horn. He disregarded the warning and accelerated into traffic, without even turning to look at the incoming vehicle.
