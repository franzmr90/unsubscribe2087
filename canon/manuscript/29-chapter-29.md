# Chapter 29
**Monday, January 26th, 2015**

John's alarm clock went off promptly at 6:00am.

It did not go anywhere.

It sat on the dresser and beeped at him, politely, from a distance of four feet, like an appliance in a hotel.

John lay in the dark and listened to it.

After about twenty seconds he got up, crossed the room, and turned it off with his finger, which is a thing he had not done since March of 2012.

He picked it up and turned it over. The wheels spun freely. The battery compartment was full. He set it back down on the dresser and pressed the test button and it went off again immediately and the wheels turned and it drove three inches and stopped and sat there buzzing, and then it drove another two inches and stopped again.

Something in it had been told to wait, and then told to wait again.

John turned it off.

He stood in his bedroom in the dark for a while in a t-shirt, at 6:02 in the morning, holding a plastic toy that he had bought in 2011 for nineteen dollars because he could not get out of bed on his own.

She had not broken it. If she had broken it he could have thrown it away.

A shower.

Two cups of coffee.

A bowl of cereal.

A bagel.

Some vitamins.

31 minute drive.

He did not count the steps from the deck to the door, on purpose, as a kind of protest, and got to his floor and discovered he had counted them anyway without deciding to, and that the number was 347, and that this was somehow the worst part.
