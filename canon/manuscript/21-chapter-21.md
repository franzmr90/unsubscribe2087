# Chapter 21
The mahogany door to Beats' office slammed open. He rounded Natalya's desk and glared at her with an air of vengeance she had never seen before. She restrained the urge to ask him what was wrong; this was not the face of a man worth questioning.

"I'm leaving," he bellowed angrily.

"What should I tell people who call while you are out?"

"Tell them to go to hell," Beats scoffed. With that, he thundered away.

John was sitting at his desk when Beats stormed up unannounced.

"I want Phonix live before you go home!" he roared at John.

"But sir, with all due respect, that could take me all night."

"You can sleep when you're dead then, John. You hear me? Don't pussyfoot around any longer, I want to see results. Be in my office with good news by five, or don't go home until it's done." Beats stormed off before John had time to respond.

A knot quickly tightened in John's stomach. He felt degraded, unappreciated, and belittled. Perhaps he just wasn't meant to finalize the project. Doubt swarmed his mind and ate at his confidence. Perhaps all his hard work was doomed to fail. Perhaps it was fate; maybe his program had never been meant to exist. Some would say he was crossing boundaries, playing God.

Regardless, he had limited time to make his creation a reality…
