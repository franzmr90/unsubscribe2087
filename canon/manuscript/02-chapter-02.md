# Chapter 2
Natalya walked away from John's office and made her way back toward her own desk on the eighth floor, and she was not thinking about him at all for the first ninety seconds, which is about average.

This seat would soon become her new home, and bring with it the security and comfort of routine. It was unsettling.

Then she was thinking about him. The way he had said "Freeman, John Freeman" reminded her too much of a James Bond movie, and she laughed out loud a little while walking, alone, in a stairwell.

He was not the most attractive guy. He had, however, managed to make her laugh at ten minutes to eight in the morning, which nobody at this company had yet done at any hour, so she did not write him off.

And he had reviewed her file. He had said so, and then heard himself say it, and gone slightly grey around the mouth.

That was interesting. Not the reviewing — everyone reviews everything. It was that he had told her.

"Hell… if nothing else I could go for a free dinner," she thought.
