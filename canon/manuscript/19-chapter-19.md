# Chapter 19
Beats sat at his desk, reading through his emails. Just then, a new email came in. The sender was "Anonymous" and the subject read "(no subject)".

"That's odd," he thought. He clicked the nameless correspondence and began to read:

> Beatus Benedikt Fassbinder,
>
> Born into wealth. Graduated Ivy League and given a company financed by your father. You have made it quite far, Beats. This marvelous pyramid you have erected and climbed is built upon the suffering of others. Through corruption and bribery you have forged an empire as countless unnamed tremble before you. Acts you believe were perpetrated in the dark have not gone unseen. Bribery. Extortion. Narcotics trafficking. This is only the beginning of the list.
>
> If you wish to remain alive to witness and participate in the metamorphosis at the end of history, you must do exactly as instructed:
>
> In the next three hours, you will make an announcement that Ubiquitous Enterprises will be turned over to employees. The fifty-four percent majority that you currently own, you will be distributing evenly between everyone at this company. You will resign from your post immediately, and never mention this message to anyone. The money currently in your bank account is more than enough to live humbly for the rest of your life.
>
> Failure to heed this warning will be your last regret. I am watching you. I do not forgive. I do not forget. Expect me.

"I do not forgive. I do not forget. Expect me," Beats read aloud as he finished the email.

Just then, the message disappeared.

He sat back. He had been threatened perhaps forty times in his life and every one of them had wanted something. This one had not asked for a dollar.

"Who in the hell does this person think he is?" Beats smashed his fist down upon his desk and yelled.

The word **SHE** flashed upon his screen.

Then his computer went black.
