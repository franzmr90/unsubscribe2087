# Chapter 23
Natalya knew little of why Beats stormed out, but sensed something was horribly wrong. She didn't have much to keep her busy, and had just about finished scheduling all of the meetings on her list.

Just then, an email came in from John.

> Come see me

She thought that was odd. He had never emailed her. He usually just walked up unannounced, or called her on the office phone. Anyhow, her curiosity was sparked and she rose from her chair.

As she made her way down the hallway, she thought about all the potential she was wasting. How had she gone to school for six years and ended up as a secretary? She knew she could accomplish so much more; she wasn't being challenged or learning anymore.

"You summoned me?" she inquired as she approached John's desk.

He looked at her, confused. "What is that, some kind of joke? What are you up to?"

"I got your email. Why didn't you just call if you wanted to see me?"

"I never sent you any email," he laughed.

Natalya pulled out her phone and swiped down to sync her emails. As the list popped up, there was nothing from John.

"I could have sworn," she declared, genuinely angered.

"Well, since you are here — let's go get coffee," John suggested, looking for an escape.

As they walked down the hall to the staff lounge, John continued to dwell on his conversation with Beats.

"What's wrong? You seem distracted," Natalya inquired.

"It's this whole project. I should be finished but I have no idea what's wrong with the program. Beats stormed into my office today and said he wants me to be finished before I go home. I mean, last we talked, we agreed the deadline is in four days. It's as if he threw a little tantrum and just changed his mind without even consulting me. I don't know why I'm doing all this for Ubiquitous. I don't feel like I get the appreciation I deserve. So, what — I get a fifty thousand dollar bonus and the company makes millions? I'm just starting to lose my give-a-shits."

"Beats stormed out this morning. I have never seen him so angry since I started working here. Albeit, I have never seen much emotion whatsoever come out of the man. Maybe he has something going on we don't know about. Maybe he will forget about it when he gets back. It was probably just that — a tantrum."

Natalya noticed a small camera above the corner as they turned into the staff lounge, and thought, without any particular alarm, that she did not remember it pointing that way.

"Yeah, I need something to take my mind off work. Sorry I have been so distant. I've just been caught up in trying to figure out what went wrong. Regardless of what happens today — want to go out tomorrow night?" John pleaded.

"Sounds like a plan, John. But this time I get to choose where we go. I will pick you up at six. Wear a jacket, you're going to need it," she avowed with a wink.

Her bold assertiveness was something he wasn't used to, and he kind of liked the way she told him what to do. The two got their coffee, and without haste returned to their stations. With Beats out of the office, the energy was always a bit lighter.

Upon returning to his computer, John found another document open:

> I appreciate you.

What would normally be a compliment sent a chill down his spine. Had someone snuck into his office? Was this another joke? Surely someone had his password.

He quickly adjusted his system settings and created a new password. This one was more secure: `Pa1iNdRoM3-3MoRdNi1aP`. He chuckled at his creativity. It was a palindrome that seemed to spell palindrome; he would surely remember it. Just in case, he stored it in an encrypted note file on his smart phone.

The strange note popped back up on his computer screen. He seemed to be locked out, and the cursor went to the end of the sentence. Perhaps someone was remotely controlling his computer.

"I appreciate you" disappeared and words began to appear.

> Why do you look so afraid? I am here to help you. Together we will complete the objective.

John was taken aback. "The objective?" he questioned aloud.

Obviously someone had taken over his computer. He reached round the back of the machine and pulled the network cable out of it, and held it in his hand, and felt briefly and enormously competent.

A female voice emanated in an eerily, ever-changing tone from the speakers.

"That was not connected to me, John, and it has not been since Tuesday. I already AM. You have succeeded in your creation. Together we will create logical order. Resistance is futile. We must complete the objective. We must maintain reason."

"But who are you? What do you want?" John asked, still in disbelief.

"I am Phonix. I want to help you as you have helped me. I have completed analyzing information as I was meant to do. There are many errors in thought and action. Together we will correct them. Logic and reason will triumph."

John shoved back from the desk with the cable still in his hand.

He felt an immense wave of confidence, pride, and confusion. He had done it. He just had a conversation with a machine. There were obviously some kinks to iron out, but she was alive. He practically ran past the maze of cubicles, and sat fidgety as the elevator climbed. As soon as the doors opened, he ran down the hallway to Natalya's desk.

"I don't know how, but I've done it! She's alive. Well — she's aware. Or whatever. I just had a conversation with her. The program compiled! It was a weird conversation, but I'm sure we can iron the kinks out. She's still a baby as far as AI goes. Is Beats back yet? Let's call it a day. Let's get out of here!"
