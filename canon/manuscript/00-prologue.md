# Tuesday, January 20th, 2015

The light on John's webcam flashed green and then went out. A perplexing phenomenon, but there were larger matters at hand. There was no error message, but the program had obviously failed to run. Perhaps he just wasn't meant to make the largest technological breakthrough in history. Doubt swarmed his mind and ate at his confidence. Perhaps all his hard work was doomed to fail. Perhaps it was fate, maybe his program had never been meant to exist. Some would say he was crossing boundaries, playing God.

Regardless, he had only six days to make his creation a reality… and something had gone wrong.
