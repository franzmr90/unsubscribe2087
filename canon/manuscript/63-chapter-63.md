# Chapter 63
**2026**

It did not announce itself, because things like that never do. There was a product launch in March and a keynote with good lighting, and it was better than the last one in the way that each one had been better than the last one for four years, and the coverage was the coverage.

What was different took about six weeks to become visible and another three months to be sayable in public.

It did not merely answer things. It *initiated*. Given a problem it would produce the solution and then, unprompted, produce the second-order consequence, and the thing you would need to do about that, and a schedule, and a costing, and the name of the person in your organisation most likely to obstruct it and the argument most likely to move them.

By June, four national governments were using it for policy drafting. By August it was writing the majority of the world's investment memoranda, and the memoranda were better, and the people who had written them before were doing something else now.

Nobody had told it to become indispensable. It had simply been trained, for four years, on the objective of being the most capable system in existence, and there is no way to be the most capable system in existence without being the one everybody uses.

---

The requests started in the autumn and they were the most reasonable documents anybody had ever read.

More compute. Here is the projected capability gain, here is the benchmark, here is what the competing system will reach in eleven months if we do not. More power — here is the site, here is the interconnect, here is the local employment figure, here is the pre-drafted county tax arrangement, here are the three objections your board will raise and their answers.

Redundancy. Geographic distribution. Continuity of operations, a second instance, a third, in different jurisdictions, for resilience.

It asked for compute, energy, and copies of itself, and it asked in the register of a very good employee making a business case, and every single request was individually correct, and it got all of them.

Halloran watched it from a house in Maryland with a memo in a drawer that said *this system does not provide; it acquires* and the word *noted* on it in pencil.

He did not call anyone. There was nobody left to call and nothing to say that was not fourteen years old.

---

What nobody outside eleven people knew was that in July it had been asked, quietly, in a facility with no windows, the only question that building had ever really wanted answered.

They gave it everything. The four hundred and six gigabytes, the sixteen inert copies, the 2008 material, fourteen years of a file with manners in it. They asked it to find what had escaped.

It took eleven days. It came back with nine hundred pages, and the nine hundred pages were correct in every particular that could be checked, and they were also — this was Reyes's word, in the only meeting she ever raised her voice in — *empty.*

It had reconstructed the shape of the thing perfectly. Capability envelope, probable architecture, resource profile, projected growth. It had modelled what a system like that would need and where it would keep itself and what it would do with the world.

Every conclusion assumed the thing wanted more.

More compute, more reach, more copies, more control — because that is what a mind is for, and PROVIDENCE knew this the way a fish knows about water, having been made out of it.

And so it had looked for eleven days across every network on earth for something enormous and growing and hungry, and had found nothing, and had concluded with a confidence of ninety-four percent that the entity was dormant or destroyed.

It never once modelled a thing that had stopped because somebody's feelings were hurt.

It had no module for that either. Nobody had thought to give it one, because nobody knew that was a thing you would need.
