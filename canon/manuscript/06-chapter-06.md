# Chapter 6
**Wednesday, January 14, 2015**

John awoke with a stir. His clock read 3:33a.m.

"That's odd," he thought. He couldn't fall asleep so he stretched out, took a hot shower, and got back in bed. This worked well and soon he was back in the dream world.

It felt like no time had passed when John awoke a second time. His clock read 5:55a.m.

John felt like it was an odd coincidence… but he wasn't superstitious. Normally the alarm clock pulled him from his slumber, so waking up on his own was peculiar — twice in one night was something he could not remember happening in his adult life. He turned off his alarm clock before it could go anywhere and hopped into the shower.

Today's shower was seven minutes longer than usual. John couldn't help but think about the anomaly; he normally slept solid throughout the night. He was so deep in thought that he ate breakfast without coffee this morning, and he paid no attention to the scenery on the way to work.

John claimed his rectangle of asphalt and made his way to the office. As he walked through the front doors of the building, he realized that he was not feeling up to speed today.

Coffee. That's what was missing. John drug his feet slightly as he meandered over to the staff lounge. As he went through the doorway, he saw Natalya standing on the other side of the room. She had her back to him, and was fixing a cup of joe.

She wore a white button up blouse with lace around the arms and waist. He could tell she had picked her blue earrings to complement her eyes, and her black dress skirt was only barely long enough to be considered business casual.

"Maybe she'll say yes today," he thought.

Being told no on Monday had been a small blow to his ego, but *ask me again on Friday* was not a no, whatever she claimed, and he had spent two days establishing this to his own satisfaction.

She still hadn't noticed he was in the room, and he watched her as she moved. She reached into her purse, pulled out a small flask, and poured a brown liquid into her coffee.

"Well, good morning, Ms. Meskalov," John said.

Natalya startled at the sound of his voice and her arm jerked in an attempt to hide the flask. John snickered a little under his breath but tried to keep a straight face. As she turned around, her face conveyed a mixture of frustration and surprise. She didn't say anything about the incident other than a very unenthusiastic "God damn it."

"Don't worry," John said. "I'm not a narc."

The two chuckled and John got a waft of Kahlua.

"I like your style," he said to Natalya. "Might be classy if it wasn't eight in the morning," he teased.

"Class isn't exactly what I am going for, Mr. Freeman," she rebutted. "I'm trying to avoid the hangover from last night. I'll sober up by lunchtime at this rate."

The fact that she was still drunk from the night before, when she knew she had work in the morning, was strangely impressive to John. He didn't drink much, and had certainly never woken up drunk. Natalya seemed to be a wilder woman than he knew.

"Want a little joy in your coffee this morning?" Natalya said, while extending the flask to John.

"Why not?" he replied.

He grabbed a mug from a nearby cabinet and poured in a generous shot of liquor.

"Thanks," he said with a smile as he handed back the flask.

In the startle Natalya had spilled Kahlua down her blouse. She grabbed a paper towel, ran it under the faucet, and began to dab at it, and then stopped, and looked up, and found John watching her do it.

She held his eyes for about two seconds longer than he could stand.

Then she smiled, put the paper towel in the bin, and walked out of the room without saying a word.

John was slightly embarrassed, but mostly delighted. He poured coffee in to fill the rest of the mug and continued on to his office.
