## Why the files are `.md`

Markdown is just plain text with a couple of simple marks: `#` starts a heading,
`**bold**` makes bold, `>` indents a quoted block. You can open these in any text
editor, and they convert cleanly to Word or PDF whenever you want a copy to read
or send. Nothing is locked in a proprietary format.

## A note on the chapter numbering

The original draft numbers chapters 1–9, then 12, 13, 15, and 18. The chapters in
between aren't missing — they open with a large decorative first letter and no heading,
so the numbers just never got typed. I've numbered them straight through 1–23 here.
Chapter 18 in the original was very long and contained five of these unheaded breaks;
it's split into 18–23 in these files. Check whether you agree with those splits.
